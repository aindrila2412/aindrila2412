Welcome to my corner of GitHub. I work in technology consulting, program delivery, and training, and I spend my free time building developer communities in India. This page is written the way I like to work with others: clear, written down, and easy to pick up without a meeting.

## What I work on

- **Program and project delivery.** Coordinating cross-functional work, aligning stakeholders on goals, timelines, and milestones, and keeping risks and status visible. Most of this has been technology consulting for clients at EY and Cvent.
- **Training and enablement.** I'm currently a Business Process Trainer at Accenture: process audits, data deep dives, workshops, and knowledge-sharing sessions that turn findings into something a team can act on.
- **Community.** Co-organizer at Google Developer Groups (GDG) Kolkata, where I helped organize DevFest Kolkata. Founded and lead the first Google Developer Student Clubs (GDSC) chapter on my campus, teaching 1,000+ students through free workshops. Microsoft Learn Gold Student Ambassador. Google Cloud Champion Innovator (recognized among the top 173 globally).

## How I work

- **Write it down.** Short written updates (what's done, what's next, what's blocking) so people in different places and time zones can catch up on their own schedule.
- **Surface blockers and risks.** Name them plainly and bring options, not just the problem.
- **Ask, then iterate.** Share a rough draft early and improve it from feedback.
